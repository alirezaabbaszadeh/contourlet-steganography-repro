\section*{S4. Metric definition}
For a payload of length $B$, the effective unrecovered bit rate is
\[
\mathrm{EUR}=\frac{1}{B}\sum_{i=1}^{B}\mathbf{1}[\hat b_i\ne b_i].
\]
Thus EUR $=0$ denotes exact payload recovery and EUR $=1$ denotes failure of the complete recovery criterion. CRC status, layer recovery, PSNR, SSIM, runtime, memory, and all recorded failure states remain in the raw evaluation objects.
