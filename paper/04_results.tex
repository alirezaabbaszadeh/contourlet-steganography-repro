\section{Results}

\subsection{The preregistered execution completed without operational failure}

The final run completed all \FinalEmbeddings{} embeddings and \FinalEvaluations{} channel evaluations (\FinalTasks{} planned tasks) under the frozen \code{FINAL-5J-v1} contract. The main matrix contained 50 pairs and seven methods; the two incremental sweeps used the preregistered 10-pair subset. The final dispatcher reported \code{run\_complete\_local} with \FinalWorkers{} workers. Analysis was built without an incomplete-run override and contained all \FinalEvaluations{} raw rows, zero missing rows, zero invalid rows, and \code{partial\_analysis=false}. Pair-level uncertainty used \FinalBootstrap{} bootstrap repetitions and aggregated repeated attack seeds within pair, method, and condition before inferential comparison.

This completion result is distinct from scientific recovery. A method can complete operationally while producing a valid scientific failure under the frozen decoding contract. No operational failures occurred in the final run.

\subsection{Clean validity was method dependent}

The clean condition exposed a shared internal boundary that was absent from the earlier pilot. C0, C1, C2, C3\_NP, and C3 each completed \InternalCleanComplete{} clean pairs. The same preregistered pair, \code{coco-000000479126-000000199771}, reached the typed S4 header-failure state for all five internal methods. B1 completed \BOneCleanComplete{} clean pairs. B2 completed \BTwoCleanComplete{} clean pairs; the remaining pairs were retained as S5 extraction/transform scientific failures under the frozen clean-valid candidate contract.

Across the attacked main-matrix conditions, none of the internal C-family methods achieved complete end-to-end recovery. JPEG qualities 90, 70, and 50, Gaussian variances 5, 10, and 15, and salt-and-pepper densities 0.01, 0.03, and 0.05 terminated at S4 for the internal methods. B1 retained header-valid partial payload under the attacked conditions (S2), whereas B2 split between S2 and the same S5 prerequisite-failure partition. Figure~\ref{fig:5j-failure-stages} shows the resulting stage distribution.

\subsection{C3 did not improve complete recovery over C0, but its raw BER was slightly lower}

The preregistered C3--C0 comparison found no difference in complete-recovery rate (Holm-adjusted $p=\CThreeCZeroCompleteHolm$). For raw BER, \CThreeCZeroBERN{} pairs had defined paired values. The mean C3-minus-C0 difference was \CThreeCZeroBERDiff{}, with a 95\% pair-cluster bootstrap interval from \CThreeCZeroBERCILow{} to \CThreeCZeroBERCIHigh{} and Holm-adjusted $p=\CThreeCZeroBERHolm$. Thus the measurable C3 advantage over C0 in this operating regime was a small BER reduction, not movement of the complete-recovery boundary.

C3 and C3\_NP also tied on complete recovery (Holm-adjusted $p=\CThreeCNPCompleteHolm$). Their raw-BER difference was \CThreeCNPBERDiff{} across \CThreeCNPBERN{} defined pairs, with Holm-adjusted $p=\CThreeCNPHolm$. The frozen data therefore do not show a detectable complete-recovery or BER benefit attributable specifically to Base-first placement.

\subsection{B1 and B2 expose a complete-validity versus partial-payload trade-off}

Against B1, C3 showed essentially the same complete-recovery endpoint (mean difference \CThreeBOneCompleteDiff{}, Holm-adjusted $p=\CThreeBOneCompleteHolm$), but the mean C3-minus-B1 payload-correct difference was \CThreeBOnePayloadDiff{} across \CThreeBOnePayloadN{} defined pairs, while the raw-BER difference was \CThreeBOneBERDiff{}. B1 also had substantially lower runtime, although the two methods implement different transport structures and this computational difference should be interpreted together with functionality rather than in isolation.

Against B2, C3 had a complete-recovery mean higher by \CThreeBTwoCompleteDiff{} (Holm-adjusted $p=\CThreeBTwoCompleteHolm$). On the \CThreeBTwoPayloadN{} pairs with defined shared payload/BER endpoints, C3 had a payload-correct difference of \CThreeBTwoPayloadDiff{} and a raw-BER difference of \CThreeBTwoBERDiff{}. These directions are not contradictory: complete validity, partial payload fraction, and BER measure different failure properties and must be reported together.

\subsection{Payload and PSNR sweeps moved BER but not the attacked complete-recovery boundary}

\input{paper/generated/table_sweep_summary}

The preregistered sweeps provide a descriptive boundary check on the internal methods. Reducing raw payload from 75\% toward 25\% lowered attacked BER, but no attacked sweep cell reached complete recovery. Likewise, stronger embedding at the 40.0~dB target produced lower attacked BER than the 47.5~dB target, yet complete recovery under the three sweep attacks remained absent. These observations show that both payload size and distortion budget affect bit-level margin, but the tested incremental operating points were insufficient to cross the strict end-to-end validity boundary.

Clean sweep validity was also informative. The shared internal clean-failure pair persisted through most incremental operating points, while C3\_NP reached complete clean recovery on all 10 sweep pairs only at the 40.0~dB PSNR point. Because this is a descriptive sweep result rather than a new preregistered primary comparison, it is not used to replace the main-matrix inference.
