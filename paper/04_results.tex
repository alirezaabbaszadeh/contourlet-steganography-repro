\section{Results}

All 16 embedding objects, 64 mandatory evaluations, and 24 conditional hard checks completed. The archive contains 88 immutable research objects and 1,582 files; the full execution table is reported in Supplementary Table S1.

\subsection{Clean decoding established transport validity}

All sixteen clean streams decoded correctly. This first confirmed that the payload fitted the selected coordinate pool and that embedding and extraction reconstructed the same slot map. It also showed that inverse transformation and uint8 quantisation at the locked PSNR target did not break the transport before a channel was applied.

Clean success is a necessary control rather than evidence of robustness. Without it, attacked failures could be dismissed as a general implementation defect. With it, the experiment isolates the channel as the step at which valid recovery was lost.

\subsection{C3 increased Base-layer protection}

Figure~\ref{fig:baseadvantage} summarises the strongest positive result. C3 shifts a larger fraction of the fixed body transport toward Base and replaces every weak Base codeword with RS(255,127). The nominal Base correction budget rises from 2,752 to 4,160 symbols. The gain is structural and verified by boundary tests; it does not depend on a favourable attacked image.

C3 also writes Base before Detail in bands ordered by adaptive score. Its advantage is therefore twofold: stronger coding and earlier access to the coordinates favoured by the calibration policy.

\subsection{The tested channels still exceeded the recovery threshold}

The larger design margin did not change the binary end-to-end outcome. Every medium and hard attacked stream produced \(\eur=1\), regardless of method or traceability pair. Figure~\ref{fig:outcomes} shows the family-level pattern: clean streams were valid, while no tested JPEG, Gaussian, or salt-and-pepper stream passed the complete integrity check.

This outcome does not erase the coding gain. It locates the tested operating point beyond the region in which that gain was sufficient. A single unrecoverable header or essential layer codeword invalidates the complete payload, even when other blocks have been corrected. The stored Base BER, Detail BER, and corrected-symbol records now provide the basis for a separate diagnostic analysis of how close each method came to the threshold.
