\subsection{Clean-path validity}

Every clean C0--C3 case decoded successfully. This establishes several facts simultaneously: the fixed transport fits the selected coordinate pool; the transform write--read path is executable; coefficient mapping is consistent between embedding and extraction; Base and Detail coding, scrambling, interleaving, and decoding round-trip correctly; and the PSNR-constrained quantised stego images preserve a valid payload without attack.

Clean success does not establish channel robustness, but it rules out a general transport or implementation failure as the explanation for attacked results.

\subsection{Verified Code-Level Base-Layer Protection Advantage}

C3 provides a measurable and verified protection advantage over C0 at the Base-layer coding level. Its protected share rises from 50.0\% to 60.2\%, its nominal symbol-correction budget rises from 2,752 to 4,160, and all Base codewords use the stronger RS(255,127) profile. In addition, C3 places Base before Detail in bands ordered by adaptive score. The Base advantage is therefore both coding-based and coordinate-based.

This is the strongest positive performance-related result supported directly by the implemented design. It is an increase in designed and unit-tested correction capability, not merely a descriptive label such as ``more protected.''

\subsection{End-to-end channel outcome}

Despite stronger Base protection, every tested medium and hard attacked case produced \(\eur=1\) for all methods and traceability pairs. No valid complete payload was recovered under the locked attack conditions. The data therefore do not demonstrate an end-to-end C3 robustness advantage at the selected payload, PSNR target, transform interpretation, and channel severities.

This conclusion does not contradict the code-level result. Error-control coding has a bounded correction region. Once one or more critical codewords exceed that region---or when the protected header becomes unrecoverable---additional redundancy can be insufficient to restore the complete stream. A design can possess a larger correction budget while both compared methods remain beyond their successful decoding thresholds.

The aggregate validity criterion is deliberately strict. A configuration that preserves more Base bits but loses one essential codeword remains an end-to-end failure. The recorded Base BER, Detail BER, and corrected-symbol telemetry should therefore be analysed in a subsequent, separately reported diagnostic study to determine whether C3 moved closer to valid recovery even when it did not cross the final boundary.
