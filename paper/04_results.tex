\section{Results}

The experimental programme completed all sixteen embeddings and all eighty-eight scheduled evaluations. The archive contains 1,582 files, with the complete execution table reported in Supplementary Table S1.

The results tell a consistent three-part story. The transport worked in every clean case; C3 delivered the intended structural advantage for Base under the common payload and quality budget; and the selected attacked conditions lay beyond complete-payload recovery for every configuration.

\subsection{Clean recovery confirmed the complete transport}

All sixteen clean streams decoded successfully, giving \(\eur=0\) in every method--pair combination. This result confirms the whole path rather than only the embedding equation: layered payload construction, Reed--Solomon coding, deterministic coordinate selection, PDFB embedding, inverse transformation, uint8 quantisation, semi-blind extraction, and final integrity validation all remained mutually consistent.

The fixed payload fitted the independently writable coordinate pool, and sender and receiver reconstructed the same slot map. Exact recovery also survived the complete image-formation boundary at the common \SI{45.0\pm0.1}{dB} cover--stego target. These clean cases establish the operating point from which channel effects can be interpreted.

\subsection{C3 delivered the intended Base-layer advantage}

Figure~\ref{fig:baseadvantage} presents the strongest measured design gain. C3 directs a larger share of the protected body toward Base and replaces every weaker Base codeword with RS(255,127). The nominal correction budget consequently rises from 2,752 to 4,160 symbols, a 51.2\% increase.

Placement reinforces the coding decision. Once adaptive coordinate groups are ranked, C3 writes the complete Base stream before Detail. The perceptually dominant layer therefore receives stronger codewords and earlier access to the coordinates preferred by calibration.

\subsection{The comparison retained a common operating budget}

Each configuration carried the same 222,360 protected bits. Its global embedding strength was selected independently, but the realised stego image had to remain within the shared PSNR interval after inverse transformation, clipping, rounding, and uint8 conversion.

The comparison is therefore controlled in two practical ways: no method hides less information, and no method is allowed more visible distortion. Differences arise from the organisation of coding and placement. For C1 and C3, adaptive scores determine capacity-safe integer quotas and bounded local weights; the resulting coefficient maps are deterministic and identified by SHA-256 digests.

\subsection{The attacked conditions located the recovery boundary}

Every tested JPEG, Gaussian, and salt-and-pepper stream produced \(\eur=1\), and the same family-level pattern continued in the predeclared hard checks. Figure~\ref{fig:outcomes} summarises the operating map: clean recovery was universal, whereas the selected attacked conditions remained outside the region of complete-payload validity.

This outcome separates design margin from end-to-end validity. C3 gives Base more protection, but complete recovery still depends on the header and every required payload component. One essential codeword beyond its correction radius can therefore invalidate a stream that still contains many corrected blocks.

Because all clean cases succeeded, the attacked boundary cannot be attributed to insufficient capacity, mismatched coordinate maps, or a general transform failure. The archived codeword outcomes, corrected-symbol counts, layer errors, CRC states, and failure stages preserve the information needed to examine how each configuration approached that boundary.
