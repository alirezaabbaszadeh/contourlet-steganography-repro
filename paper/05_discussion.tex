\section{Discussion}

The study produced a clear separation between structural protection and complete channel recovery. C3 measurably increased the protection available to Base and placed that layer earlier in the highest-ranked coordinate groups. The tested attacked conditions nevertheless remained beyond the point at which this additional margin was sufficient for complete-payload validation.

This distinction is scientifically useful. It shows that stronger layer-aware coding can be implemented and verified independently of whether a selected channel severity falls inside the final recovery region. The experiment therefore did more than return a binary outcome: it identified which part of the system became stronger and located a channel region in which further protection or a more flexible validity policy is required.

Several characteristics of the experiment help explain this result. The \SI{45}{dB} quality target limits coefficient displacement, while the 222,360-bit stream occupies a broad coordinate pool. Complete validity also depends on the protected header and the required payload layers. A single essential codeword beyond its correction radius can therefore reject the complete stream even when many other blocks have been corrected successfully.

Unequal protection intentionally exchanges some Detail redundancy for additional Base redundancy. Its most direct benefit may consequently appear first in layer-specific outcomes rather than in an all-or-nothing complete-payload measure. A separately validated Base layer could reveal meaningful partial reconstruction even when Detail is unavailable.

The completed run provides a strong platform for investigating that possibility. Every result is connected to a fixed image pair, method, transform fingerprint, coefficient map, embedding object, attack realisation, and evaluation object. New severity levels can therefore extend the threshold analysis without replacing or obscuring the present evidence.

The experimental discipline is another strength. The payload was not reduced after observing the results. The PSNR target was not relaxed. Image pairs were not replaced, and additional channel settings were not selected to manufacture a favourable comparison. The final evidence consequently preserves a transparent account of the original operating point.

Three positive conclusions emerge. First, the complete transport is operational: all sixteen clean streams survived the full path from layered payload construction to integrity-validated secret recovery. Second, hierarchical protection is measurable: C3 increased the nominal Base correction budget by 51.2\%, increased the Base share of the protected body to 60.2\%, and upgraded every weaker Base codeword. Third, the study established a reproducible recovery boundary that can guide a finer severity sweep and layer-specific validation in the next experiment.

That boundary is unusually inspectable. Each row is linked to one pair, method, transform fingerprint, attack realisation, embedding object, and evaluation object. Future experiments can therefore test a new threshold, payload rate, or validity rule while preserving the history and identity of the current evidence.

\section{Limitations and Future Work}

The empirical evidence is intentionally bounded to four fixed traceability cases. The explicit PDFB profile is an independently specified implementation rather than an author-equivalent reconstruction, and extraction remains semi-blind. Geometric robustness and steganalysis are outside the final factorial experiment.

These boundaries point directly to productive extensions. The next experiment should sample channel severity more finely between clean recovery and the present medium settings. Plotting corrected-symbol distributions, codeword margins, header validity, Base and Detail BER, layer CRC states, Base-only validity, and complete-payload validity against channel severity would reveal where each protection mechanism begins to contribute.

A separately protected Base integrity field is a particularly promising extension. It would allow the system to distinguish complete Base-and-Detail recovery, valid Base recovery with unavailable Detail, and insufficient integrity for any reconstructed output. This layered validity model would align the decoding decision more closely with the hierarchical coding architecture and could reveal whether the increased Base protection preserves a recognisable lower-fidelity secret before the complete payload becomes recoverable.

The current transform-bound calibration also creates opportunities for further development. Stability could be estimated across finer severity intervals or expanded with coordinate-level reliability features while retaining strict separation between calibration and reporting cases. The independently writable P3+P4 construction likewise provides a foundation for exploring alternative payload rates, stronger header protection, compact metadata, and different Base/Detail redundancy allocations without changing the underlying transform interpretation.

The repository includes deterministic diagnostic and representative-panel exporters so that these extensions can be connected to the same run identity and source objects. Direct comparison with the source article should follow once the transform, payload, pairing, quantisation, extraction, attacks, and metrics have been harmonised.

\section{Conclusion}

This study develops a hierarchical and validity-aware digital transport for contourlet-domain image steganography. The architecture protects the information that contributes most strongly to the secret image through two complementary mechanisms: stronger Reed--Solomon coding and priority placement in adaptively ranked multiscale coordinates.

C3 increases the nominal Base correction budget from 2,752 to 4,160 symbols, a gain of 51.2\%, while raising the Base share of the fixed protected body from 50.0\% to 60.2\%. These improvements are achieved without reducing the payload or relaxing the realised cover--stego PSNR target.

All sixteen clean streams completed the full transport and integrity-validation path successfully. The tested JPEG, Gaussian, and salt-and-pepper conditions remained beyond complete-payload recovery, establishing a clear and reproducible threshold for future refinement.

The resulting contribution is broader than a single channel comparison. It combines perceptually informed payload organisation, hierarchical coding, transform-bound adaptation, independently writable PDFB coordinates, self-describing validity, and interruption-resilient evidence into one inspectable system.

Most importantly, the study provides a constructive path forward: finer threshold mapping, stronger protection of critical metadata, and independently validated Base recovery can build directly on the verified architecture presented here.
