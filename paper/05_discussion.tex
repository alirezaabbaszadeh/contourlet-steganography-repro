\section{Discussion}

The central result is a separation between architectural priority and strict end-to-end recovery. C3 allocates stronger protection to Base and gives that layer first access to adaptively ranked coordinates, but the final paired analysis found no improvement in complete recovery over C0. The measurable C3--C0 difference was instead a small reduction in raw BER. A structural increase in protection margin therefore did not guarantee movement of the strict validity threshold at the tested operating point.

The C3--C3\_NP comparison sharpens that interpretation. These methods share adaptive allocation, unequal protection, quotas, weights, and coefficient maps and differ only in Base-first versus alternating layer order. They tied on complete recovery, and their BER difference was not detectable after multiplicity correction. Base-first placement is consequently an architectural property of C3, not an empirically demonstrated source of complete-recovery gain in FINAL-5J.

The external controls reveal a different trade-off. B1 had essentially the same complete-recovery endpoint as C3 but retained much more partial transmitted information and had lower raw BER. B2 showed a different limitation: under its frozen clean-valid candidate contract, 18 of 50 preregistered pairs were scientifically clean-infeasible. C3 therefore exceeded B2 on complete recovery, while the direction of partial-recovery metrics depended on the endpoint. These comparisons argue against reducing robustness to a single scalar.

The typed failure model is consequently part of the scientific result. The internal methods shared one preregistered clean pair that failed at the header stage, while B2 had a broader clean-prerequisite limitation. Those outcomes were retained rather than removed or replaced with favorable pairs. Scientifically unreachable dependent cells remained explicitly not evaluated, and unavailable quantities were not imputed as zeros.

The payload and PSNR sweeps reinforce the boundary interpretation. Reducing the transmitted fraction lowered BER, and stronger embedding at the lower target PSNR also lowered BER. Nevertheless, none of the perturbed-channel sweep cells reached complete recovery. The isolated 10/10 clean result for C3\_NP at 40~dB is useful descriptive evidence, but it does not replace the preregistered main comparison or justify post-hoc selection of a more favorable operating point.

The 50-pair design also changes the meaning of the earlier pilot. The small pilot suggested universal clean validity for the internal transport, whereas FINAL-5J exposed a common clean-failure pair and method-dependent baseline feasibility. Expanding the study therefore did more than increase precision: it revealed behavior absent from the small traceability set.

All 530 embeddings and 8,420 planned evaluations were materialized under the frozen plan with zero operational failures, and the final analysis contains zero missing and zero invalid rows. This makes the mixed and negative scientific findings interpretable rather than attributable to an incomplete execution or silent row loss.

\section{Limitations and Future Work}

The scope of inference is the 50 preregistered image pairs and the frozen digital channel matrix. The study does not establish performance for geometric transformations, arbitrary image populations, or channel families outside the evaluated JPEG, Gaussian, and salt-and-pepper conditions. The internal C-family and the two engineering controls also use different recovery architectures, so runtime and implementation complexity should be interpreted together with functionality.

B1 and B2 are harmonized engineering controls rather than author-equivalent reproductions of external publications. Their common information, distortion, channel, and metric contracts make them useful comparators, but they do not reproduce every implementation choice of their source repositories. Some paired endpoints have fewer than 50 defined pairs because scientific prerequisite failures make those quantities genuinely unavailable; the reported paired sample size is therefore part of the result rather than a value to be imputed.

A subsequent protocol should investigate the shared internal clean-failure path, including header robustness and the clean image-formation boundary. Such work requires a new protocol revision and run identity rather than retroactively modifying FINAL-5J. Finer information-load or distortion settings outside the present 25--100\% and 40--47.5~dB sweep ranges may map the transition to complete recovery more precisely, but those settings should be preregistered before outcome inspection.

Further extensions can examine alternative redundancy allocations, separately strengthened metadata, additional receiver designs, and broader image/channel conditions. The existing layer-integrity format already distinguishes complete recovery from valid Base-only recovery; future experiments should exploit those states under conditions where Base-only validity occurs often enough to support meaningful pair-level inference.

\section{Conclusion}

FINAL-5J evaluated a layered contourlet-domain transport through a preregistered 50-pair, seven-method experiment comprising 530 embeddings and 8,420 channel evaluations. The execution completed with zero operational failures, but the scientific findings were deliberately not simplified into a universal robustness claim.

C3 did not improve complete recovery over C0, although it produced a small and consistent reduction in raw BER. C3 and C3\_NP did not differ detectably on complete recovery or BER, so the final experiment provides no evidence that Base-first placement alone shifted the tested boundary. B1 preserved substantially more partial transmitted information while remaining similar on complete recovery, whereas C3 had higher complete recovery than B2 because B2 was clean-infeasible on a subset of preregistered pairs. Payload and PSNR sweeps changed BER without producing complete recovery under the tested perturbed-channel conditions.

The principal contribution is an endpoint-aware and reproducible account of layered hidden-image transport. Semantic Base/Detail organization, explicit multiscale coordinates, mechanism-resolved comparisons, harmonized controls, typed scientific failures, pair-level inference, and content-addressed provenance are combined in one system. The result identifies what the hierarchy changes, what it does not change at the frozen operating point, and where a subsequent preregistered study should search for the next recovery boundary.
