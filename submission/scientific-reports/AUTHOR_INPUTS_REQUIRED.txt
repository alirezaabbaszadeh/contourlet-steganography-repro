Scientific Reports — author-controlled inputs still required

1. Institutional affiliation.
2. Corresponding-author email.
3. ORCID, if used.
4. Funding statement: funder/grant identifiers, or explicit confirmation of no external funding.
5. Final confirmation of the CRediT-style Author Contributions statement.
6. Acknowledgements, if any.
7. Confirmation that the manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere.
8. Suggested reviewers, if desired.
9. Reviewers to exclude, if any, with reasons.
10. Prior discussion with a Scientific Reports Editorial Board Member, if any.

Do not infer these values from Git history, public profiles, or account metadata.
